# Disclosure

This Disclosure sets out the information the record publishes, the information it withholds, and the
basis on which it withholds it.

---

## 1. Status

The Program trades the proprietary capital of reout, which is not registered with any financial
regulator.

The Program is traded only in the accounts listed in the account register. The operator does not
trade the Program, or a strategy substantially the same as it, in any other account, under the name
reout or under any other name, and does not publish another record of it. The operator gives
institutional allocators and independent verifiers a written attestation of this paragraph. An
account at another venue in which the Program is traded is added to the account register before it
is used (Methodology, section 7).

Nothing in this document or its repository constitutes an offer, a solicitation or investment
advice. The Program's signals are not sold, and no investors are solicited. The Program is not
available to individual investors. Other activities of the operator are set out in section 6, and
contact details in section 5.

Information is withheld where it would disclose the Program's current exposure or its
implementation.

## 3. Disclosure of the method

Information on the strategy's decision process is disclosed at three levels.

| Level | Content | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Category | Systematic, directional, single-instrument, long and short; signals generated by a self-supervised, memory-based model | Public |
| Thesis | The inputs on which the model is conditioned, the source of the expected edge and the reasons it is expected to persist | Institutional allocators and independent verifiers under NDA |
| Implementation | Model, development data, parameters, gates and execution rules | Not published. An independent verifier under NDA may inspect the sealed configuration solely to confirm it against its pre-registered hash |

Public materials on the strategy, including Monthly Reports and methodology notes, are confined to
the strategy category and its measured properties, such as drawdown and correlation with the
Benchmark. They do not describe the thesis.

Each deployed version is developed on data ending before it is deployed. All live results are
therefore out of sample.

## 4. Incidents

Every operational incident and every manual action is logged and disclosed as the Methodology,
section 5 sets out, whether or not it affected P&L. The logs are not edited.

## 5. Contact and identity

Institutional and verification inquiries: `ops@reout.io`.

The Program is operated under the name reout. The identity of the operator and its legal form are
given on request.

The official channels of reout are the domain `reout.io`, the GitHub account `reouthq`, of which
`reouthq/track` is the only public repository, and the X account `@reouthq`. Any other website,
account or address purporting to represent reout is not affiliated with it.

## 6. Other activities

The operator may conduct other businesses, including research tools and data offered by
subscription, under the name reout or under other names. None of them receives the Program's
positions, signals or configuration. Those under the name reout are listed in this section when
they begin. The others are disclosed to institutional allocators and independent verifiers on
request.
