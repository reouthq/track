# Reviews under the Risk Limits

Every level of the Risk Limits reached from record inception, and the finding of the review it
started, entered when the review concludes and reported in the monthly report for the month in which
the level was reached (Risk Limits, section 4). Entries are not edited: an outcome announced later
is entered as a new row that refers to the review.
