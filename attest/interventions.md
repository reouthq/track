# Manual interventions

Every manual action on the designated account(s) from record inception, meaning any action not
taken by the system itself, is logged here at the time it occurs and reported in the monthly report
for that month. Entries state the action, its reason and its effect at the level of category; quantities and
prices are in the private log available to verifiers (Methodology, section 6).
