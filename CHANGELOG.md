# Changelog

This changelog records every amendment to the Methodology, the Disclosure, the Risk Limits and
the account register, together with its rationale. Each amendment is hashed and anchored before it
applies to any report. Prior reports are not recalculated under a later methodology.

| Date (UTC) | File | Change | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-30 | all | Initial versions, pre-registered before record inception | `attest/2026-09-30-preregistration.md` |
| 2026-10-25 | METHODOLOGY.md | Sections 2 and 3: each external cash flow is valued at its time and splits its valuation interval, from the interval ending 2026-10-22. That interval, first published with its flows treated as occurring at the start of the interval, is corrected in the log of corrections, the published values retained. Rationale: a flow treated as occurring at the start of the interval takes the whole interval's result as earned on the capital after the flow. On 2026-10-22, 84.6% of NAV was withdrawn after most of the day's loss had been incurred on the whole account, and the daily return was published as −27.65% | `attest/2026-10-25-amendment.md` |
| 2026-11-04 | DISCLOSURE.md | Sections 2 and 3: research notes may report findings on research practice and on markets, in a form that discloses neither a program's thesis nor its implementation, nor a candidate change to a program; the thesis and implementation behind any finding, experimental results that would identify a program's inputs, models, parameters, gates or a candidate change, and development data remain withheld. Rationale: reout is a research firm, and findings on research practice and on markets can be published without disclosing a program's thesis or implementation; the previous wording withheld all experimental results, which was broader than the purpose stated in section 2 | `attest/2026-11-04-amendment.md` |
